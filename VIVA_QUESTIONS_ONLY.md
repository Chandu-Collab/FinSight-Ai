## Preparation Tips

### Tips for Viva Preparation:
1. **Understand the basics** thoroughly before moving to advanced topics
2. **Practice explaining concepts** in simple terms
3. **Be honest** about what you don't know
4. **Connect answers** to your project implementation
5. **Prepare examples** from your project to illustrate concepts
6. **Stay calm** and think before answering
7. **Ask for clarification** if questions are unclear

### Key Areas to Focus On:
- **Frontend**: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, authentication
- **Backend**: Flask, REST APIs, security, database integration
- **Database**: Schema design, relationships, indexing, RLS
- **Machine Learning**: Linear Regression, feature engineering, model evaluation
- **General**: Project overview, problem-solving, communication skills

### Answer Structure Tips:
- Start with a direct answer
- Provide relevant examples from your project
- Explain the "why" behind your decisions
- Mention trade-offs and alternatives
- Be concise but thorough
- Show enthusiasm for your work

Good luck with your viva!
